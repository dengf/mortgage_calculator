Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

Mortgage Calculator has no server, no accounts, and no analytics. The figures you type are never transmitted anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account to create, no form that submits anywhere, and no tracking or analytics of any kind — no cookies, no pixels, no third-party scripts.

How the calculations work

The calculation engine is compiled from Rust to WebAssembly and runs inside your browser. Loan amounts, rates, incomes, debts, CPF balances and every other figure you enter are processed on your own device and are never sent over the network.

Saved scenarios

Saving a scenario writes it to your browser's IndexedDB storage — local to that browser on that device. It is not synced, backed up, or transmitted. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it permanently, and we have no copy.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub's infrastructure handles the network requests needed to deliver the page and may log technical details such as IP addresses. That is standard web hosting and is outside our control; it is covered by GitHub's privacy statement. We receive no data from it and have no access to those logs.

Beyond loading the page itself, the app makes no network requests. You can verify this in your browser's developer tools: open the Network tab and use the calculators — nothing is sent.

Open source components

The calculator is built from open-source software — the Rust crates compiled into the WebAssembly engine, and the JavaScript libraries that render the page. Their licenses require that their copyright notices travel with the code, so the full license text for every one of them is on the open source licenses page. None of them is a network service: they are compiled into the files your browser already downloaded.

Children

The app is a general-purpose financial calculator and is not directed at children. Since no data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be published here with a new date above. Because the app collects nothing, any change that introduced data collection would be a significant one and called out in the app itself.

Contact

Questions or corrections: open an issue on GitHub.
